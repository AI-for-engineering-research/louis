2. Findings, read physically

Who they are. The offending patches (green_N, n=1852, lat ≥45°N, 98% cloud_both — not placement error) are broken-to-overcast supercooled mixed-phase decks at ~850 hPa over Labrador Sea / Hudson Bay / snow-covered Quebec: obs CTT 254 K [250,258], LWP 21.5 / IWP 11 g/m², τ_liq ≈ 7 ≫ τ_ice ≈ 0.3, liquid r_e (Martin) 6.1 µm, the "ice" being folded snow at 856 hPa / 260 K (grey 91 µm). The correct dark-brown contrast (brown_N) is the opposite: deep ice shield, IWP 190, τ_ice 4.1, TCC 1.0. The sim paints the decks G ≈ +4.5 K (saturating the composite green) where GOES shows ≈ +1.5 K.

Why it's the liquid. Three binned splits (per §5's rules): dG equal across snow/water/land surfaces (not 8.5 µm surface emissivity); dG rises with TCC (+2.1→+3.3 — not a clear-sky cover leak; dC14 shows the opposite gradient, so the warm bias is cover, the green is not); dG rises with τ_liq and falls with icefrac. Then the two attribution reads: green_N dG is invariant (+2.3…+3.2 K) across all eight frozen-particle configs — ice r_e 9→91 µm, snow-class k2 both reffs, MH97 single/bimodal — while the CONSTLIQ bracket swings it +3.51 → −1.23 K (4→30 µm), leaving brown_N untouched (dG −1.1, dC14 identical −4.8). The lever is surgical: it moves exactly the wrong population and nothing else. Zero-crossing ≈ 14–23 µm vs the population's Martin ~6 µm. Per Mayer 2024, smaller particles → larger G at moderate τ — the same small-particle green mechanism, here in the liquid phase.

3. Ruled IN / ruled OUT

  • Ruled IN — liquid droplet size is the particle knob for the light-green clouds: Martin-with-constant-N_d (50/300 cm⁻³, the ERA5/CY41R2 values; martin_liquid.py flags this simplification itself) under-sizes these high-latitude winter decks ×2–3.
  • Ruled OUT — every frozen knob for this population (invariance matrix), surface emissivity (equal dG across surfaces), cover-leak (wrong TCC gradient), placement (98% cloud_both).
  • Ruled OUT — 30 µm as a config: scene G overshoots negative in every CTT bin; its RGB 0.1272 (< champion 0.1308) is the saturating secondary metric — do not promote a bracket.
